# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Augplot welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing discussion on what is best for the project and its users.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing when mistakes affect others, and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project.
Project spaces include the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other communication channels used by the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a conduct concern, open an issue requesting a private contact channel without
including sensitive details. Maintainers will review reports promptly, respect the
reporter's privacy, and respond in a manner proportionate to the circumstances.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact,
and patterns of behavior.
